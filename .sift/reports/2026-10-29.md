## Summary

The owner's standing rule is that nothing a model, a grader or a trace sees is cut, unless a provider documents the limit. The code breaks it in 134 places. Each removal changes what a model reads, which the closed set of audit edits forbids, so every confirmed item is recorded as `decide`. The owner has already given the direction. The fix goes on one product branch, checked against the live ship check.

Main groups:

- **The observation the agent acts on** (`ObservationLimits`):
  - Viewport text and working notes are cut at 6,000 characters.
  - Controls are cut at 320 in the pool, 120 offscreen and 160 offered.
  - History keeps 6 full entries and 14 short ones. Visited addresses keep 20.
  - Choice options stop at 240, though Jev documents 255.
  - Jev's documented 32k and 64k token limits are budgeted at 75 percent.
- **Output caps still sent to providers:** plan 8,000 tokens, verdict 400, shortcut address 200. These are the caps the earlier output-cap change missed.
- **Page capture** (`capture.js`, `snapshot.js`):
  - Labels, alt text, link text and nearby text are cut at 256 or 1,024 characters.
  - Observation blocks stop at 64 controls, 32 images and 32 links.
  - Hover discovery stops at 40 targets, 200 rules and 400 checks.
  - Form text stops at 2,000 characters, control context at 120.
- **Reading and memory:**
  - Reads hold at most 60 continuing records; past that, claims are dropped and the reader is never told.
  - Excerpts stop at 2,000 characters, context blocks at 2,000, tally examples at 3.
  - Read spans stop at 320 characters, effect summaries at 4 names of 60 characters.
  - Counterevidence stops at 4,000 characters, repair text at 8,000.
  - Downloads stop at 16 KiB each and 64 KiB in total.
- **Hidden rules that override a model without telling it:**
  - A playback word list skips Jev's irreversible-action question.
  - Recovery rewrites the model's give-up into done, and its done into read.
  - A lookup click Jev chose is dropped once code thinks the plan is answered.
  - `maxItems` is removed from strict schemas but still enforced.
  - A traversal word list overrides the planner and verifier.
  - Failed links are filtered from what Jev sees.
